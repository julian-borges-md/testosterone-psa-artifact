# Phase 5 prespecification (S2 collider test)
Logged: 2026-10-11T23:36:49Z before computation.
S2: conditioning on PSA may be mediation or collider bias. Negative-control logic: a generic collider distortion would move exposures regardless of their PSA effect. Prespecified reading: evidence favours mediation if the exposure that raises PSA attenuates substantially more than the exposure with no PSA effect; evidence favours collider distortion if all exposures move similarly.
Result: bioavailable testosterone (raises PSA) attenuated 77%; IGF-1 (no PSA effect) attenuated 27%; BMI (lowers PSA) moved 103% but from an imprecise univariable estimate (p=0.26) and is uninformative. Reading: weakly favours mediation; does not exclude a collider component. Reported as such.
