# Figure alt text and legends

The target journal requires alt text for every figure, entered in the submission system and carried in the main document. Alt text describes what a reader who cannot see the figure needs in order to follow the argument; it is not a repetition of the legend.

## Figure 1. The complete estimator matrix for PSA

**Alt text.** Three side by side forest plots, one for each exposure, showing the effect on prostate specific antigen in natural log units per standard deviation. Each plot lists the same seven estimators down the vertical axis, from inverse variance weighted at the top to MR RAPS at the bottom, with a horizontal scale running from minus 0.2 to 0.2 and a dotted vertical line at zero. For bioavailable testosterone, six of the seven estimates lie to the right of zero with intervals that exclude it, the exception being MR Egger, which lies to the left with an interval crossing zero. For total testosterone, most estimates sit close to zero, two excluding it on the positive side and MR Egger excluding it on the negative side. For SHBG, six of the seven estimates lie to the left of zero with intervals that exclude it. Filled markers mark intervals excluding the null and open markers intervals containing it.

**Legend as printed.** Effect of each exposure on PSA in natural log units per standard deviation, for all seven estimators and all three exposures. Filled markers indicate a 95 percent interval excluding the null and open markers an interval containing it; bars are 95 percent confidence intervals. Values are taken from the same committed matrix as Table 2.

## Figure 2. Randomized trial evidence and the Bayesian synthesis

**Alt text.** Two panels. Panel A is a forest plot of relative risks from randomized trials of testosterone therapy, on a logarithmic horizontal scale labelled 0.5, 1, 2 and 5 with a dotted vertical line at 1. Prostate biopsies sit above 1 at about 2.4 with an interval running from just above 1 to beyond 5. Prostate cancer sits below 1 at about 0.85 with an interval spanning 1. Panel B plots the posterior odds ratio for any prostate cancer against the assumed therapeutic shift in standard deviations, from 0.5 to 3.0. A grey curve for the genetic evidence taken at face value rises from about 1.07 to about 1.39 and crosses a red dashed line at 1.2 marking meaningful harm. A blue curve for the genetic evidence with the PSA channel removed stays flat and slightly below 1 across the whole range. Shaded bands show the 95 percent credible intervals, which are wide and overlap 1 throughout for the blue curve.

**Legend as printed.** A, testosterone therapy in randomized trials: prostate biopsies and prostate cancer. B, posterior odds ratio for any prostate cancer by assumed therapeutic shift, with and without the PSA channel.
